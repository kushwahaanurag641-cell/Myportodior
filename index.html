Alpha #101: Volume-Weighted Mean Reversion

Type: Reversion & Liquidity Dynamics
Factor Expression (Formula):
rank(ts_decay_linear(correlation(vwap, volume, 10), 5)) * -1 + rank(close - ts_min(low, 20))

Economic Hypothesis & Mechanics:

When large institutional volume drives prices to local extremes without sustained multi-day momentum, liquidity providers step in, causing a mean-reversion drift over the next 3 to 5 trading horizons.
